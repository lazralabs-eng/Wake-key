The shape of the book

Seventeen chapters in four books, built on Giambattista Vico's idea that history moves through an age of gods, an age of heroes, an age of ordinary people, and then a return. Joyce ends mid-sentence so the last line runs into the first. Pick a book on the ring.

The cast and their masks
